Psychology of Blame for Repeat Offenders

Brown University Capstone Project

For this capstone, I designed and administered a survey to more than 300 members of the Providence community to study how people, acting as prospective jurors, assign moral blame to repeat offenders. Drawing on behavioral psychology, cognitive-bias research, and criminal law, the study examined how a defendant's prior record shapes perceptions of fault, culpability, and the likelihood of re-offense, and how recidivism prejudice may distort factual assessments in criminal defense.
